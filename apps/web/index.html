                    About PennyWise Accounting

                    A connected accounting workspace for the people who set up the books, do the day-to-day work, and need dependable answers at month-end.

                    One place for the business record

                    PennyWise Accounting brings customers, vendors, banking activity, inventory, reports, and the accounting record into one organization workspace. It is designed to make the flow of a transaction easier to follow: enter the source document, review what it affects, keep supporting detail available, and use reports to understand the result.

                    1. Set the foundationCompany details, fiscal calendar, taxes, chart of accounts, and opening balances.
                    2. Record the workInvoices, bills, expenses, payments, banking activity, and inventory movements.
                    3. Review and reconcileMatch transactions, investigate exceptions, and protect completed accounting periods.
                    4. Report and shareUse current reports, exports, audit history, and role-based access to make decisions.

                    Build a reliable accounting foundation

                    Start with the settings that make every later transaction consistent.

                    Company setup

                    Create your organization profile, select a recurring fiscal year-end date, add tax registrations, and choose a chart-of-accounts preset. The setup flow is structured so a new organization can establish its books before operational work begins.

                    Chart of accounts and opening balances

                    Review, add, and organize the accounts that describe your business. Opening balance tools give you a controlled way to establish the starting position when moving from another system, while visible validation helps spot an unbalanced starting entry.

                    Business appearance

                    Store a company logo and invoice appearance choices with the organization. Theme, density, and text-size controls let each person use the workspace in a way that is comfortable for their screen and reading needs.

                    CAD and USD accounting

                    Keep financial statements in the organization's base currency while invoices, bills, receipts, payments, and bank balances preserve CAD or USD. Dated manual rate snapshots, separate realized gains and losses, open monetary positions, and period-end revaluation make the conversion trail reviewable. Native WooCommerce and Etsy connectors preserve each provider currency and stage activity in a non-posting review inbox.

                    Manage sales from customer to payment

                    Keep customer relationships, sales documents, and payment follow-up connected.

                    Customers, products, and services

                    Maintain reusable customer and product records so recurring work does not need to be re-entered. Active records populate operational forms, reducing accidental use of outdated contacts or items.

                    Invoices and estimates

                    Create estimates and invoices with line items, tax, notes, due dates, and customer details. The invoice designer includes an exact document preview so you can check the branded result before saving, printing, or sending it. Estimates can support a public response flow for customer approval decisions.

                    Payments and follow-up

                    Record manual payments, review payment status, and manage related refunds or disputes from the Payments area. Payment-provider settings are kept separate from the accounting workflow, making it clear where provider connection and operational review belong.

                    Run purchasing, banking, and inventory day to day

                    Operational records feed the books while keeping the original business context close at hand.

                    Purchases and payables

                    Track vendors, bills, expenses, bill payments, and vendor credits in one workflow. Payables aging makes outstanding commitments easier to see, while individual documents retain the vendor and payment context needed for review.

                    Banking and reconciliation

                    Add bank accounts, upload statement activity, categorize transactions, create matching rules, record transfers, and reconcile an account. Import review and matching tools are designed to make exceptions visible before they become a month-end surprise.

                    Inventory control

                    Keep locations, inventory products, quantities, receipts, customer returns, vendor returns, adjustments, and transfers together. Inventory views surface movements, low-stock signals, cost of goods sold, and reconciliation detail for teams that need both operational and accounting visibility.

                    Turn activity into usable answers

                    Use reports and review tools to understand what changed, why it changed, and what needs attention.

                    Financial reports

                    Run a dashboard summary and core accounting reports including profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, general ledger, cash flow, receivables, payables, sales, expense, banking, and tax views. Filter reports by date and accounting basis, then print or export the result for review.

                    Accountant review

                    Use manual journals, review queues, accounting periods, journal tracing, and audit history to investigate the record. Period controls help separate active work from periods that have already been reviewed and closed.

                    Traceable accounting record

                    The product is built around balanced entries, clear source documents, and audit events. When a correction is needed, the aim is to preserve an understandable history rather than hide what happened, giving reviewers a clearer path from a report total back to its supporting activity.

                    Move in, work together, and keep control

                    PennyWise Accounting includes practical tools for switching systems and running a shared organization.

                    Switching centre

                    Import CSV exports from common accounting systems or a spreadsheet, map columns, run a test import, review issues, and only then complete the import. The same area can prepare a complete organization export, helping you keep a portable business record.

                    Team roles and organization separation

                    Invite people into an organization with roles that reflect their work, such as bookkeeping, sales, purchasing, inventory, accounting, or read-only access. Each organization has its own workspace and records, supporting clearer boundaries for teams, clients, and separate businesses.

                    Support and data controls

                    Built-in diagnostics help gather support information without guessing. Organization export and closure controls support data stewardship, while activity history and review tools make it easier to explain the state of the books to the right people.

                    Using this build responsibly

                    PennyWise Accounting is currently a testing build. Use representative or test data while you evaluate workflows, verify reports and integrations with your accountant or bookkeeper, and complete any required payment-provider or production deployment setup before relying on it for live financial operations.

                    Recording a remittance

                    Filing is done on the authority's own return, from the figures above. Record the payment in PennyWise Accounting as a manual journal in the accountant workspace: debit the authority's payable account for the amount remitted, and credit the bank account it was paid from.
