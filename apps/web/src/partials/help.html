    Protect private information

    Do not enter passwords, access keys, bank or card numbers, payroll information, government identifiers, personal addresses, or customer and vendor records.

    What the optional diagnostics include
    • Browser and language
    • Online status and viewport size
    • Theme, density, text size, and current workspace

    They do not include names, email addresses, organization identifiers, login details, documents, account numbers, transactions, or amounts.

    Review the finished report before sharing it. An email draft has no recipient; choose a trusted support person and press Send yourself.
