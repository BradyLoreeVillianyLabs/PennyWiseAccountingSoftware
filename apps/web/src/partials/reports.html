What you file, by authority

Each authority is reconciled against the account its own tax component posts to. GST/HST and provincial sales taxes are filed separately, on different schedules.

Recording a remittance

Filing is done on the authority's own return, from the figures above. Record the payment in PennyWise Accounting as a manual journal in the accountant workspace: debit the authority's payable account for the amount remitted, and credit the bank account it was paid from. That clears the liability and leaves the journal linked to the period.

PennyWise Accounting does not file returns, calculate what is owed to an authority, or move money. The figures above come from posted documents only.
