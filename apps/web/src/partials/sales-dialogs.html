Issue a credit note

This creates and posts a full credit against the selected invoice. It does not move money.

Choose lines or amounts to credit

Select one or more invoice lines and enter a credit amount for each. The amount cannot exceed the line's remaining eligible value.

Void invoice

Voiding reverses the posted receivables journal and leaves an auditable reversal. It does not erase the invoice or move money.
