# PennyWise Accounting paid-beta marketing and sales plan

## Positioning

Use one sentence consistently:

> PennyWise Accounting helps Canadian small businesses and their bookkeepers move records into a
> reviewable CAD/USD workspace, reconcile the result, and keep the accounting trail clear.

Do not advertise “QuickBooks replacement,” automatic tax filing, payroll, live bank feeds,
or autonomous WooCommerce/Etsy posting. The product is strongest as an accountant-assisted
switching, review, and books-health workflow.

## Offer

- **Founder release:** C$99 one-time for the first 1,000 completed purchases through the
  owner-configured Stripe-hosted checkout. Do not advertise the offer until its checkout and
  final terms are configured.
- Founder access includes released PennyWise Accounting updates; it does not promise unrelated
  future products. There is no recurring PennyWise subscription for founders.
- Founder eligibility and fulfilment remain an owner-reviewed workflow. Publish final founder
  terms, refund handling, tax treatment, and availability count before accepting payment.
- Optional assisted migration can be quoted separately as a one-time service. It is not required
  to use the software and must never be presented as a monthly software fee.

## Where to sell first

1. **Accountant and bookkeeping firms:** Alberta, British Columbia, and Canadian firms with
   10–50 small-business clients. Offer a partner workspace and assisted migration for one
   client first; referrals are the primary channel.
2. **WooCommerce/Etsy operators:** Canadian sellers with recurring reconciliation pain,
   CAD/USD activity, SKU mismatch, or spreadsheet workflows. Sell review and clean handoff,
   not marketplace automation.
3. **Service businesses:** trades, agencies, consultants, and field-service companies that
   need polished invoices, bills, bank reconciliation, projects, and simple inventory.
4. **Communities:** Canadian bookkeeping associations, local chambers of commerce, Shopify/
   WooCommerce/Etsy seller groups, and accounting-software migration forums. Share a useful
   migration checklist before asking for a demo.

## 30-day launch sequence

### Days 1–7: proof and list

- Publish the WordPress landing page only after the archive and checksum are configured.
- Create a 20-account prospect list: 10 bookkeepers, 5 commerce sellers, 5 service firms.
- Send a short message offering a 20-minute workflow review, not a generic software demo.
- Record source system, monthly transaction range, current workaround, and decision maker.

### Days 8–14: assisted demos

- Use the deterministic demo company for every first demonstration.
- Demonstrate source selection, spreadsheet mapping, preview, dry run, reconciliation,
  books health, invoice preview, export, and recovery guidance.
- Do not use real customer credentials or operational store access during a first demo.
- Ask for a paid pilot only after the prospect identifies a measurable workflow cost.

### Days 15–21: paid pilots

- Accept no more than five initial pilots.
- Use a written scope and consent-safe onboarding instrument.
- Establish baseline and result for upload-to-review time, exception count, variance, close
  time, and hours saved.
- Have the customer's accountant review any tax, payroll, inventory valuation, or filing use.

### Days 22–30: references and repeatability

- Produce two permissioned case studies using ranges and workflow outcomes, never raw books.
- Convert successful pilots to founder enrolments or separately scoped one-time assistance.
- Give each referring accounting firm a tracked referral code or written referral agreement.
- Remove any claim that was not demonstrated in the release evidence.

## Message templates

**Bookkeeper:** “I’m testing a Canadian-first workspace that stages QuickBooks/spreadsheet
imports, shows the mapping and exceptions, and gives you a reconciliation handoff instead
of silently posting uncertain rows. Would a 20-minute review of one client workflow be useful?”

**Commerce seller:** “If your marketplace export, SKU list, and bank deposit do not agree,
PennyWise Accounting can stage the evidence, suggest matches, and keep unresolved items visible. I
can show the workflow with demo data before you decide whether a paid pilot is worthwhile.”

**Service business:** “PennyWise Accounting combines polished invoices, bills, bank reconciliation,
projects, and CAD/USD controls in a local-first beta. The first engagement is assisted so
we can reconcile the result together.”

## Channel rules

- Prefer referrals, direct outreach, local accounting events, and useful migration content.
- Use LinkedIn for accountant case studies and operational lessons, not unsupported claims.
- Use seller communities only with moderator permission and educational content.
- Do not buy scraped email lists, upload customer data to marketing tools, or add hidden
  analytics to the WordPress site.
- Keep a suppression list and honour Canadian anti-spam/privacy requirements after counsel
  review.

## Commercial metrics

Track weekly: qualified conversations, demos, paid pilots, setup conversion, monthly
activation, time-to-first-reviewed-batch, reconciliation variance, 30-day retention,
referrals, support hours per organization, and gross margin. The beta is ready to scale only
when at least five organizations pay, complete a core workflow, and would recommend it.
