## Proposed transaction and process

- Package: owned source, build/release instructions, tests, migrations, documentation and transferable product assets. Include domains, trademarks and customer contracts only after ownership and assignability are established. Exclude personal credentials and unrelated projects.
- Economics: seek all cash at closing; price offers by cash actually available after escrow, debt, fees and tax. Any earnout, seller note or deferred payment is excluded from the property cash budget.
- Transition: offer 60 days, capped at 40 hours total, of documented handover; negotiate additional work separately. These are proposed terms, not existing obligations.
- Days 1–7: establish an immutable source snapshot and installer hashes, reconcile local source to Git history, assemble license/IP and revenue evidence, and record a demo using synthetic data. Avoid copying secrets into the package.
- Days 8–30: proposed outreach to 10–15 selected Canadian accounting, bookkeeping technology and commerce software operators. Share a teaser first, then a controlled data room. No recipients have been contacted or expressed interest.
- Days 31–60: compare written indications, cash-at-close, conditions and completion risk. Grant limited exclusivity only against a credible funded proposal. Timing is a process target, not a sale forecast.

## Property arithmetic

At C$250,000 gross, PennyWise covers at most 8.3% of a C$3 million property price before any deductions. The gross gap is C$2.75 million, plus closing costs and retained reserves. Other real assets remain unverified.

Required gross proceeds = (property price + closing costs + retained reserve + debt and fixed sale costs − other available cash) / (1 − assumed proportional deductions). As pure sensitivity, C$3 million net requires C$3.33m, C$3.75m or C$4.29m gross at aggregate deductions of 10%, 20% or 30%, before other costs. These are not tax rates or personal tax estimates; ownership and deal structure are unknown.

Acquire.com's confirmed SaaS transactions had a median 3.9× annual profit multiple in 2024 and 2025. At that benchmark, C$3 million gross corresponds to roughly C$769,000 annual profit. This illustrates the commercial evidence gap; the benchmark does not value this beta/IP asset or guarantee an eventual multiple.
