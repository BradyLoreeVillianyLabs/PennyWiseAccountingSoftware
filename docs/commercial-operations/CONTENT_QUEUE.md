## Copy controls

- Use “may,” “is designed to,” and “we are learning” for beta positioning. Replace outcome claims such as “saves hours,” “eliminates errors,” or “compliant” unless there is dated, approved evidence for that exact claim.
- Do not present a feature as live, supported, or generally available merely because it appears in a roadmap or demo.
- Do not solicit reviews, ratings, likes, follows, reposts, or testimonials in exchange for access, discounts, or support.
- Do not use customer names, logos, screenshots, or operational data without written permission and a review of confidentiality obligations.
- The final version on the platform must match the approved copy except for formatting required by that platform; substantive edits restart review.
