# Design-Partner Outreach and Support Workflow

## Scope and guardrails

This is a small-batch, operator-sent workflow. It is not bulk email, social automation, lead scraping, or a request for engagement. Contact people only through an approved, lawful channel and only where the organization has an appropriate basis to do so. Follow the recipient's opt-out request immediately.

## Human-sent outreach draft

**Subject:** Would a short ClearLedger beta-fit conversation be useful?

Hi [First name],

I’m reaching out because [specific, truthful reason you believe their workflow may be relevant]. ClearLedger is a Canada-first accounting and invoicing beta for small businesses. We are inviting a small number of owner-operators and advisers to test workflow fit and give candid feedback on areas such as migration, invoices, expenses, inventory review, projects, and accountant handoff.

This is not a request for a review or promotion. Before any access, we would confirm whether the beta fits your workflow, what data would be involved, and how feedback would be handled. ClearLedger beta does not promise automated tax filing, statutory payroll, live bank feeds, production OCR, or fully automated marketplace order-to-payout accounting.

Would you be open to a short, no-pressure fit conversation? If not, I will not follow up.

Thanks,
[Named sender]

## Qualification and consent record

Before granting beta access, a human owner records:

| Field                                 | Record                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization / contact                | Keep in approved private system only                                                          |
| Why the contact was approached        | Specific, factual, non-sensitive reason                                                       |
| Contact channel and permission/basis  | TBD by owner and counsel                                                                      |
| Workflow fit                          | CAD/CAD+USD, migration/review needs, light inventory or service workflow, adviser involvement |
| Explicit beta expectations discussed  | Beta status, known limits, support path, feedback purpose                                     |
| Data sensitivity and least-data pilot | Use demo/non-production data first where feasible                                             |
| Access decision and owner             | Approved / declined / deferred with date                                                      |
| Opt-out / deletion request            | Capture promptly in approved private system                                                   |

## Support and feedback loop

1. Acknowledge a request within the support commitment that the owner has approved; do not promise a response time that has not been staffed.
2. Classify it: access/fit, how-to, defect, data/privacy, security, billing/terms, or feature feedback.
3. For defects, collect the minimum useful reproduction detail through the approved support channel. Never request passwords, secret keys, or a full financial export by email or social direct message.
4. For data/privacy or security reports, route immediately to the designated security/privacy owner; do not investigate in public.
5. For accounting, tax, legal, or payroll questions, state the support boundary and refer the partner to a qualified adviser as appropriate.
6. Close the loop with what was done, what is still unknown, and no unapproved roadmap or regulatory commitment.

## Feedback prompts for the first conversation

- What part of your current workflow is hardest to review or explain later?
- What needs to be staged, approved, or reconciled before you would rely on it?
- What would your bookkeeper or accountant need for a useful handoff?
- Which beta limitation would make participation impractical?
- What is the safest, smallest pilot workflow for your team?
