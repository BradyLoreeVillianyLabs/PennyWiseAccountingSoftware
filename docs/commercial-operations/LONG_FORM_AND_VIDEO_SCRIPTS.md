# Educational Content: Review Before You Rely On It

## Article draft — A practical way to evaluate accounting software during a beta

Changing an accounting workflow is not just a software decision. It changes how records arrive, how people review them, and how a bookkeeper or accountant can understand what happened later.

For a beta evaluation, start with the work that happens before a number reaches a financial report. Ask whether source information can be staged, whether a person can see and correct a mapping, and whether reconciliation is part of the normal workflow. A fast import is useful only when the team can understand what was imported and what still needs attention.

ClearLedger beta is aimed at Canadian owner-operated businesses, including light-inventory sellers working in CAD and USD, while keeping the core workflow usable for service companies. Its beta promise is a coherent workspace for migration, invoices, expenses, inventory review, projects, tax evidence, forecasting, and accountant handoff. The evaluation should focus on fit for your actual process—not on a generic promise that a tool will replace professional judgment.

Three questions make a productive design-partner conversation:

1. Which source records do you need to migrate or keep reviewing?
2. Where does a human need to approve, reconcile, or correct a result?
3. What would your bookkeeper or accountant need to see to trust the handoff?

For ClearLedger, the beta is an opportunity to test those questions in a real workflow and report where the product is unclear or incomplete. It is not a promise of automated tax filing, statutory payroll, live bank feeds, production OCR, native mobile-store delivery, or fully automated marketplace order-to-payout accounting.

If this is the kind of workflow you want to pressure-test, request a beta-fit conversation. We will first confirm scope, data sensitivity, and what a useful feedback loop would look like.

## Video script — 45 seconds: “Four questions before switching”

**On-screen:** Four questions before switching accounting tools

**Voiceover:** “Before switching accounting software, ask four questions. What is the source data? What gets staged before it is accepted? Where does a human review or correct it? And how is the result reconciled? ClearLedger beta is designed around those workflow questions for Canadian small businesses, including teams working in CAD and USD. We are recruiting a small number of design partners to test fit and give candid feedback. This is a beta—not a promise of automated filing, live bank feeds, or fully automated marketplace accounting. If you want to evaluate the workflow, request a beta-fit conversation.”

**Visual notes:** Use product-free diagrams or approved, non-customer screenshots. Show “Staged → Reviewed → Reconciled” without showing financial data.

## Video script — 30 seconds: “A smaller beta, clearer feedback”

**On-screen:** Small beta. Human feedback loop.

**Voiceover:** “ClearLedger is keeping its beta deliberately small. We want owner-operators, bookkeepers, and accountants to tell us where migration, invoices, expenses, inventory review, projects, and handoff work well—and where they do not. We will confirm fit before access and keep feedback human-led. If your business uses a reviewed accounting workflow, request a design-partner conversation.”

**Visual notes:** Avoid promises, performance metrics, testimonial-style quotes, platform branding, and any CTA that pressures a viewer to share or endorse.
