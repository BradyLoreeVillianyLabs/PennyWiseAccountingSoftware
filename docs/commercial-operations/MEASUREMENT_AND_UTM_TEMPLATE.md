# Manual Measurement and UTM Template

This template supports learning from approved activity without installing a tracker or handling credentials. Record only aggregate observations from an owner-controlled destination and the platform's normal reporting interface.

## URL pattern

`https://[owner-controlled-domain]/[approved-path]?utm_source=[source]&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=clearledger_beta_[yyyy_mm]&utm_content=[content_id]`

Example placeholders only: `utm_source=community`, `utm_medium=organic`, `utm_campaign=clearledger_beta_2026_08`, `utm_content=cl_beta_01`.

Rules:

- Use lowercase letters, numbers, underscores, and hyphens; do not put a person's name, email address, company name, account number, or sensitive category in a UTM value.
- `source` identifies a channel class chosen by the owner (for example, `community`, `newsletter`, or `partner`); it need not name a social network.
- `content_id` must match the approved queue ID. Do not reuse it after changing the substantive copy or offer.
- Do not use URL shorteners unless the owner can inspect the final destination and the shortener's privacy terms have been approved.

| YYYY-MM-DD  | CL-BETA-01 | community     | TBD          |                               TBD |                               TBD |                         TBD |                          TBD | TBD                | Continue / revise / retire |

Interpretation:

- Treat the numbers as directional observations, not proof of causality or product-market fit.
- Prefer qualified conversations and useful feedback over raw views, reactions, or follower counts.
- Keep individual prospect information in the approved CRM or contact system, subject to its privacy controls—not in this public-content log.
- Before comparing two pieces of content, confirm that audience, destination, timing, and offer were comparable enough to learn from the difference.
