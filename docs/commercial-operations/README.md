# ClearLedger Beta Commercial Operations Pack

This is a local, review-first working pack for recruiting a small number of design partners for the ClearLedger beta. It creates no accounts, sends no messages, schedules no posts, collects no tracking data, and contains no credentials or third-party integrations.

## Intended beta audience

Use this material for Canadian owner-operated WooCommerce or Etsy businesses that keep books in CAD, sell in CAD and USD, carry light inventory, and work with an owner plus a bookkeeper or accountant. Service companies may also be suitable when the owner confirms the workflow fit.

## What is safe to say

Use only claims in `CONTENT_QUEUE.md` that the owner has marked approved. Keep the beta label visible and describe workflows rather than outcomes that have not been evidenced. Do not claim automated tax filing, statutory payroll, live bank feeds, production OCR, native mobile-store delivery, guaranteed marketplace availability, or fully automated order-to-payout accounting.

## Owner runbook

1. Select one item from `CONTENT_QUEUE.md`; set its owner, target date, destination URL, and status to `Ready for review`.
2. Verify each product claim against the current release evidence and complete `REVIEW_AND_MODERATION_CHECKLIST.md`.
3. Have the named approver sign off on the exact copy, creative, CTA, and URL. Retain the approved version and date in the queue.
4. Create a unique, privacy-respecting URL from `MEASUREMENT_AND_UTM_TEMPLATE.md`. Use an owner-controlled destination only; do not add pixels, SDKs, or unapproved trackers.
5. In an approved platform scheduler, connect only an organization-owned account, paste the approved copy, attach approved media, choose the reviewed time, and use that exact approved URL. Keep scheduler permissions limited and revoke access when an operator changes roles.
6. Before publishing, re-check the platform's current advertising, disclosure, and automation rules. Publish only after a human confirms the final preview. Never use bulk posting, bought engagement, scraping, auto-comments, auto-follows, or impersonation.
7. Record only aggregate, platform-visible results in the measurement template. Route replies through the support workflow; do not move personal data into the queue unless the prospect has intentionally supplied it through an approved channel.

## Package map

- `CONTENT_QUEUE.md` — reviewed-copy queue and claim guardrails.
- `LONG_FORM_AND_VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md` — one educational article and two short video scripts.
- `REVIEW_AND_MODERATION_CHECKLIST.md` — approval, response, and escalation controls.
- `MEASUREMENT_AND_UTM_TEMPLATE.md` — manual attribution and learning log.
- `DESIGN_PARTNER_OUTREACH_AND_SUPPORT.md` — human-sent invite, intake, and support loop.

This pack is operational guidance, not legal advice. The release owner is responsible for current product evidence, required disclosures, privacy notices, applicable marketing law, and each platform's then-current rules.
