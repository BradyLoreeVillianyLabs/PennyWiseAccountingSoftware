# Review, Moderation, and Boundary Checklist

## Required approval before publishing or scheduling

- [ ] Named owner, publisher, and approver are recorded in the queue.
- [ ] Exact copy, visual, CTA, landing destination, and publication window are approved together.
- [ ] Product statements are verified against current release evidence; unsupported promises were removed.
- [ ] The content clearly identifies ClearLedger as beta where relevant.
- [ ] The destination has a current privacy notice and a clear, non-misleading next step.
- [ ] Any personal data, customer story, logo, screen, or testimonial has documented permission.
- [ ] UTM values identify the campaign without encoding a person's name, email, account, or sensitive attribute.
- [ ] A human reviewed the final in-platform preview and current platform rules.
- [ ] No automated engagement behavior, scraping, deceptive identity, bulk unsolicited messaging, or paid/fake engagement is involved.

## Comment and direct-message triage

| Situation                                            | Operator response                                                                                                      | Escalate to                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Fit question                                         | Use approved beta positioning; invite the person to the approved fit-conversation link.                                | Commercial owner if a new promise is requested.    |
| Product support question                             | Acknowledge, avoid diagnosing in public, and route to the approved support channel.                                    | Support owner.                                     |
| Security, privacy, or data deletion concern          | Do not request account details publicly. Acknowledge and route through the approved private support channel.           | Security/privacy owner immediately.                |
| Tax, legal, payroll, or accounting advice request    | State that ClearLedger does not provide professional advice; suggest their qualified adviser.                          | Compliance owner if a product claim is implicated. |
| Abuse, harassment, impersonation, or suspected fraud | Preserve the link/screenshot and use the platform's human moderation/reporting controls.                               | Trust/safety or account owner.                     |
| Feature request or defect report                     | Thank the person, capture only the minimum necessary details in the approved feedback channel, and make no commitment. | Product/support owner.                             |

## Operator rules

- Do not argue, delete criticism merely for being negative, or manufacture consensus. Remove or report content only under documented platform/community rules.
- Do not ask for financial records, passwords, tokens, or account identifiers in comments or direct messages.
- Do not promise a roadmap date, support level, regulatory result, provider integration, or account outcome.
- Keep a simple moderation log: date, platform, public/private, category, operator, action, escalation, and resolved date. Restrict access to authorized staff.
- Review local law, privacy obligations, consent requirements, anti-spam rules, and platform terms with qualified counsel before any outreach or paid promotion. This document is not legal advice.
