## ClearLedger decisions informed by this source

1. Prioritize reliable reconciliation, complete report drill-down, invoicing,
   accounts-payable review, migration, auditability, and accountant collaboration.
2. Keep the initial Canadian WooCommerce/Etsy seller wedge as the practical launch
   beachhead, while retaining a longer-term fit for service businesses, contractors,
   professional firms, and light-inventory companies in Canada and the United States.
3. Make migration and reversibility product capabilities. Preserve source evidence,
   show variances, support dry runs, and never trap a customer's export behind a plan.
4. Prefer capacity- and service-based packaging. Core accounting correctness,
   reconciliation, reports, audit history, period close, accountant access, tax coding,
   data export, and security controls belong in every paid plan.
5. Treat accountants and bookkeepers as the highest-trust acquisition channel, followed
   by high-intent migration/reconciliation content, referrals, reviews, and targeted
   search.
6. Keep AI as an enabling layer inside controlled workflows, never an autonomous posting
   product or a substitute for sound accounting models.
7. Defer full-service payroll, manufacturing, complex consolidation, and automated tax
   filing until compliance, specialist support, contracts, and release operations can
   sustain them. Build payroll-ready records and filing-ready reports first.

## Current product implications

ClearLedger already has strong local foundations in double-entry accounting, AR/AP,
file-based bank reconciliation, reporting, immutable audit history, controlled period
close, projects, light inventory, budgets, CAD/USD accounting, source-labelled imports,
and review-only WooCommerce/Etsy evidence.

The most important remaining commercial gaps are accepted order-to-payout accounting,
complete source-native migration kits and cutover evidence, reviewed OCR, an accountant
portfolio workflow, managed production hosting and support, and validated live-provider
operations. U.S. tax localization and payroll are later regulated programs, not launch
toggles.
