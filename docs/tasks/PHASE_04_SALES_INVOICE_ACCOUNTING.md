# Phase 4: Sales foundation and invoice accounting

The authoritative Phase 4 task is the owner-provided task attachment. This repository copy records the phase boundary for traceability: customers, products and services, draft invoices, deterministic tax totals, approval/posting, credit notes, void/correction workflows, receivables queries, authorization, APIs, minimal UI, and automated tests.

Phase 5 invoice rendering, PDF generation, email delivery, public invoice viewing, payments, purchases, banking, inventory quantities, and dashboard work are explicitly out of scope.
