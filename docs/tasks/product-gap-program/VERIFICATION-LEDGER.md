# Verification ledger — every claim the pack relies on

Checked 2026-09-11. This ledger supersedes the "still unverified" list in
`REVIEW-panel-critique-2026-09-11.md`. Every claim the prompts rely on is listed here with
its evidence grade.

## Evidence grades

| Grade   | Meaning                                                                                                                                               |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **P**   | **Primary.** Read directly from official vendor, government or standards documentation, a statute, or PennyWise source code.                          |
| **P\*** | **Primary, via summary.** An official page, but its body could not be retrieved by our fetcher; the content comes from a search summary of that page. |
| **S**   | **Secondary.** A reputable third party: a law or accounting firm, review aggregator or trade press. Used only where no primary source was reachable.  |
| **V**   | **Vendor self-report.** Who said it is verified; the claim itself can't be independently tested.                                                      |
| **O**   | **Open.** Not answerable from public sources. The dependent prompt contains a test that settles it before release.                                    |
